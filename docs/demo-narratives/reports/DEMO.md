# Reports — retained outputs and evidence

**Outcome:** Show the outputs produced from searches, graph context, jobs, and
assistant interactions without losing provenance.

**Readiness:** The prepared demo has graph/search results or retained job output.

## Talk track and clicks

1. Open **Reports** after completing the selected workflow.
2. Identify the output type and originating context.
3. Review graph, search, or companion output with its source references.
4. Confirm timestamps, identifiers, and applicable quality status.
5. Export or share only through the customer-approved path.

**Evidence to call out:** originating request/run, report type, source IDs,
generation time, quality state, and retained artifact.

**Key message:** Customer outputs preserve enough context to be audited and
reproduced.
