# Recommendations — explainable engineering guidance

**Outcome:** Present quality, reuse, manufacturing, and impact recommendations
grounded in graph context.

**Readiness:** A graph entity with sufficient connected evidence is selected;
recommendation services are ready.

## Talk track and clicks

1. Open **Recommendations** for the prepared entity.
2. Separate detected facts from recommended actions.
3. Review impact, reuse, and manufacturing guidance.
4. Open the supporting entity or path for one recommendation.
5. Explain confidence, missing evidence, and the human decision required.

**Evidence to call out:** recommendation category, affected entity, supporting
relationships, score or severity, and source evidence.

**Key message:** Guidance is explainable and reviewable; it does not silently
change governed data.
