# ReqIF — requirements digital thread

**Outcome:** Demonstrate requirements ingestion, structure, relationships, and
traceability into the product knowledge graph.

**Readiness:** A non-confidential ReqIF package is available; ingestion and graph
services are ready.

## Talk track and clicks

1. Open **ReqIF** and load the approved package or prepared result.
2. Review specifications, requirement types, attributes, and hierarchy.
3. Select a requirement and inspect its source identity and relationships.
4. Follow a trace to the linked model or product entity.
5. Show missing-link or validation findings as actionable quality evidence.

**Evidence to call out:** ReqIF identifier, specification hierarchy, attributes,
relation type, source artifact, and linked graph entity.

**Key message:** Requirements retain their exchange semantics while joining the
cross-domain digital thread.
