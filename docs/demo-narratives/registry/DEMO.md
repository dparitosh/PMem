# Metadata Registry — governed assets and vocabulary

**Outcome:** Show a searchable control plane for ontologies, dictionary terms,
data products, and lifecycle state.

**Readiness:** Registry assets are seeded or created through approved ingestion;
catalog and ontology services are ready.

## Talk track and clicks

1. Open **Metadata Registry** and refresh.
2. Move through registry views to distinguish ontology, dictionary, and governed
   asset records.
3. Select an ontology source and filter terms by prefix or business text.
4. Open an asset to show ownership, version, provenance, and lifecycle state.
5. Perform a lifecycle transition only when the demo role is authorized.

**Evidence to call out:** stable identifier, source namespace, owner, version,
status, timestamps, and linked artifact or graph reference.

**Key message:** Discoverability and lifecycle control sit above raw storage.
